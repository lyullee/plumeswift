FLADIS 9, 16 and 24 / equivalent-vapor benchmark

Inputs
  SMEDIS v2.4 spreadsheets: https://admlc.com/smedis-dataset/
  JRIII protocol: https://admlc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/jriii-initial-modeling-exercise-description-v2.4.pdf
  Site geometry: https://admlc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/fladis-test-site-geometry-and-wind-directions-v1.pdf
  Gexcon submission: https://admlc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/flacs_gexcon_lmauri.zip

  Place fladis9.xls, fladis16.xls and fladis24.xls from SMEDIS batches 3, 2
  and 2 respectively in tmp/ammonia_benchmarks, with the Gexcon ZIP named
  flacs.zip. Use the JRIII protocol for source and wind inputs where workbook
  summaries differ. Coordinates come from each workbook. Trial 16's source
  is 2 m upstream of the array origin. The model uses flat unobstructed ground,
  consistent with the JRIII baseline terrain option. Upstream building wakes
  and explicit low-frequency wind meander are omitted. The surface-layer GLE
  uses the prescribed mean direction, Monin–Obukhov length, friction velocity
  and measured crosswind velocity variance, without concentration fitting.

  Inject the published fully vaporized top-hat handoff at the Table 4
  axial location, 1.5 m height, temperature, speed, and 12 mol% NH3.
  Keep the tabulated NH3 mass flux by reconciling the disk diameter with
  the ideal-gas mixture at site pressure (9: 0.88 -> 0.933 m; 16: 0.73 ->
  0.739 m; 24: 1.069 -> 1.066 m). Temperature and concentration set the
  injected sensible enthalpy through the air/NH3 ratio. Transport uses the
  existing buoyancy, drag and shallow-layer pressure closures with NH3/air
  molecular weights and fixed gas heat capacities. The model does not solve
  the upstream liquid jet or include NH3 absorption, deposition, humid-air
  phase change or explicit ground heat exchange. Gexcon's final runs do
  include ground heat exchange.

Comparison
  Sample the 20, 70 and far arcs for the JRIII 600, 600 and 400 s windows
  starting at simulated release onset. The exact start of each window in
  the original time series was not available in the spreadsheets. The far
  arcs are reported together under 240 m. JRIII lists actual radii 238 m
  for Trials 9/24 and 240 m for Trial 16, while SMEDIS labels them 235.5 m
  and Gexcon submits the far fixed-height maxima at 238 m for all trials.
  Use the actual workbook coordinates for each measured sensor, including
  additional vertical levels. Exclude non-arc auxiliary sensors.

  Fixed-height arc scans use z=0.1, 0.5 and 1.5 m, with lateral increments
  1, 2 and 4 m respectively. The receptor averaging boxes are 2x2x0.2,
  4x4x0.4 and 8x8x0.6 m. Report three maximum definitions in the CSV:
  the published observed arc maximum, the maxima over the paired sensors
  at the reference height, and the maximum from the model's arc scan.
  On the 70 m arc the workbook has no 0.5 m center receptor, but the
  published observed maximum can be from another height (FLADIS 24's
  2610 ppm at z=0.1 m). Width is lateral concentration-weighted standard
  deviation using trapezoidal integration, both over the model arc scan
  and over identical sensor positions for the measured/model pair.
  Published SMEDIS width is also shown as a separate reference.

  For FLACS, use the latest submitted provided-source (CERC) maxima at
  measurement heights, Pasquill D as the common reporting reference.
  Report the range across alternate E (Trial 16) and C (Trial 24)
  submissions in the CSV. Gexcon does not publish receptor-wise predictions
  or plume widths in this archive, so those comparisons use measurements.
  The result is a cross-model benchmark with differing wind/mixing/heat
  treatments, not a controlled comparison of the numerical solvers.

Reproduce from the repository root in PowerShell
  $env:PYTHONPATH=((Resolve-Path python_qes).Path,(Resolve-Path tmp).Path,(Resolve-Path tmp/smedis_deps).Path) -join ';'
  $env:OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS='1'
  $env:NUMBA_CACHE_DIR=Join-Path (Resolve-Path tmp).Path 'lh2-cryogenic-numba'
  .venv/Scripts/python.exe results/fladis_benchmark/run_fladis.py
  .venv/Scripts/python.exe results/fladis_benchmark/compare_fladis.py
