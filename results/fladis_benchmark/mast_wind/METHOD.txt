FLADIS 9, 16, 24 mast-wind direction audit

Inputs are from SMEDIS v2.4 fladis9.xls, fladis16.xls and fladis24.xls,
section Wind_Direction_Sensor. The four rows per trial are measured means
at 10 m above ground. Keep the SMEDIS survey convention, +x at 110 degrees
clockwise from grid north, meteorological from-directions, and +y
counterclockwise from +x. The along-axis from-direction is 290 degrees.
The physical survey origin is distinct from Trial 16's release point at
(-2, 0, 1.5) m. The PlumeSwift grid translates every x by +20 m and y
by +104 m, including the station x locations; the reported coordinates
remove that translation. Sensor coordinates and benchmark scan positions
are paired with the previous run. The nominal far arc includes 238 and
240 m; the archive's actual surveyed sensor locations are used for the
paired results.

The earlier JRIII case-wide means were 296, 278 and 281 degrees. Use the
measured station means at x = -6.5, 70 and 227.5 m instead. For the last
station x, average the two flanking bearings on the circle with equal
weights. Interpolate bearings linearly in x and hold the nearest value
outside the station range. At each height, retain the original mean
longitudinal wind and set v/u = tan(290 degrees minus the station bearing).
This avoids introducing a longitudinal mean divergence from turning the
flow. The earlier zero-mean time perturbation, thermodynamics, source,
wind speed profile for u, turbulence covariance, 120 particles/s, 0.25 s
step, seed and concentration averaging windows are reused. The measured
direction varies across the final arc, especially in Trial 24. The averaged
far station therefore represents a simplified wind field, not a measured
2-D or time-varying field. No concentration measurement is used to choose
or weight the wind inputs.

Center is the concentration-weighted y centroid evaluated on identical
observed sensor locations at the reference height. Width and peak use
the same fixed-height benchmark arc scan as the original and are compared
with the published SMEDIS width and maximum. The paired-sensor widths and
peaks are also provided, including the 70 m height distinction in Trial 24.
The Gexcon CERC-source FLACS reference here is the Pasquill D
fixed-height peak in requested_arcmax_conc_meashts.txt. The C/E envelope
is retained. The archive provides arc maxima, not a spatial receptor
profile from which to calculate comparable centers or widths. Geometric
drift change is the integral of the mean v/u difference from handoff
to the nominal arc, a diagnostic without turbulent transport.
