FLADIS 9 / 16 / 24: vertical integration, water thermodynamics and marker self-density
Date: 2026-11-11

Question
  Diagnose persistent overprediction downstream of 70 m without fitting
  turbulence, heat transfer, wind direction or material parameters to sensors.

Frozen baseline
  results/fladis_benchmark/lateral_wind/pps120
  Includes measured mast means by x and y, divergence-compensated lateral
  mean field, existing synthetic low-frequency wind sequence, measured sigma_w,
  existing MOST dissipation and GLE, ground h=20 W/(m2 K), dry ideal NH3/air.
  That wind sequence matches summary variability, not measured time series.

Same conditions
  NH3 rates 0.40 / 0.27 / 0.46 kg/s; averaging/release windows 600 / 600 / 400 s.
  Seed 20261110, 120 markers/s, main dt=0.25 s with adaptive substeps.
  Grid cells 2 x 2 x 0.25 m, force reconstruction volume 1 m3.
  Same source disks, speeds, positions, receptor coordinates/volumes, mast
  observations, ground heat, drag and shallow-layer coefficients in every run.
  No concentration-based optimization or selected-trial coefficient changes.
  Original source temperature 203.7 / 203.9 / 204.0 K, NH3 12 mol%.

Independent changes
  midpoint: existing stochastic midpoint scheme; no new turbulence constants.
    Stationary-covariance analytical check solves the discrete Lyapunov equation
    for the exact numerical map (rather than estimating variance from sensors).
    Homogeneous covariance is preserved; this alone does not prove the
    inhomogeneous well-mixed condition or time-step convergence of trajectories.
    In fact, the analytical discrete velocity autocorrelation sum gives the
    SAME homogeneous long-time diffusivity for BE and midpoint. Therefore a
    variance deficit alone must not be presented as proof of deficient mixing.
  moist: dry NH3 gas + humid air with water vapor/ice/liquid equilibrium.
    Workbook RH is 86%, 0.62 and 0.536 respectively; normalized to percentages
    by the workbook label, not by dispersion outcomes.
    Close pressure, NH3 mass, inferred dry-air/water loading and energy together.
    Include vaporization, condensation, freezing/melting and sensible heat.
    Nonzero condensate mass contributes to mixture density; condensate volume
    is neglected. Root domain 160--400 K; invalid states fail explicitly.
    Binary NH3-water dissolution/activity/excess enthalpy is NOT implemented.
    No aerosol deposition or independently transported water scalar is added.
  loo: optional leave-one-out cell-mean density for buoyancy/pressure only.
    Remove one average Monte Carlo marker's species mass and enthalpy before
    evaluating the FORCE density. Keep ALL mass and energy in thermal and
    material budgets and in concentration observers. Count=1 gives ambient
    force density; large counts approach the ordinary estimator.
    Cell-mean approximation requires equal marker masses and approximates
    differing enthalpy histories by their cell mean. Unequal masses fail.
    This removes the known m/V self-contribution; nonlinear-density sampling
    bias remains possible. It is an experimental diagnostic, NOT a substitute
    for marker-count/grid convergence and NOT enabled as a production default.

Run matrix
  before: archived frozen baseline (not rerun, used as supplied reference).
  midpoint: dry, original source enthalpy, midpoint only.
  moist: moist, original dry source enthalpy, backward Euler.
  moist_midpoint: moist, original dry source enthalpy, midpoint.
  source_moist_midpoint: moist, midpoint, rederive source enthalpy from the
    SAME prescribed 12 mol% and source temperature in the humid closure.
  loo_moist_midpoint: preceding source-consistent case + leave-one-out forces.
  Every new mode is run for ALL three trials, not just the worst trial.

Source convention is critical
  Original dry h: -1.2434 / -1.2551 / -1.2683 MJ per kg NH3.
  Humid source h at the SAME source T and 12 mol%:
    -1.5951 / -1.5246 / -1.5181 MJ/kg.
  Keeping old dry h in the humid EOS instead gives source equilibrium T
    220.35 / 216.70 / 215.85 K, rather than the prescribed 203.7--204 K.
  Those fixed-h runs are energy-controlled ablations, not matching-source-T
  validation. Do not report their apparent improvement as an equal-source test.
  Tabulated sources themselves were inferred, not thermodynamic time-series
  measurements; this source ambiguity cannot be removed by tuning receptors.

Independent diagnostics
  stationary_covariance.csv: exact homogeneous GLE stationary covariance.
  thermodynamic_states.csv: density, temperature, condensate, acceleration,
    NH3 self-virial correction estimate at its PARTIAL pressure. This virial
    estimate is a dilute-gas diagnostic, not a full NH3/air mixture EOS bound.
  source_conventions.csv: source energy and temperature consistency.
  marker_resolution.csv: one-marker force-cell floor and acceleration.
    Dry one-marker concentrations are 4553 / 3095 / 5297 ppmv. They are NOT
    sensor detection limits: sensors average over space and time and can
    predict much lower means. The issue is instantaneous force reconstruction.

Comparison rules
  sensor_comparison.csv: 153 identical receptors for each configuration.
  arc_comparison.csv: maxima, lateral widths, paired-sensor centers at nominal
    20, 70 and 240 m. Actual far radii are 238, 240, 238 m as before.
  metrics.csv: sensor MAE (including zero observations), NMSE, FB, FAC2 over
    positive observations only, center/width MAE and peak geometric error.
  FLACS D is the published CERC-source submission; alternate E/C min/max are
    included. FLACS receptor fields, vertical profiles and widths are NOT
    available in that archive, so do not fabricate corresponding comparisons.
  The published 70 m observed peak can come from a different vertical level
    than the fixed z=0.5 m model arc; paired-receptor MAE avoids this ambiguity.
  Multiple jobs share CPU/RAM; wall times are provenance, not a controlled
    timing comparison with the archived baseline or FLACS.
  A common seed is recorded, but changed paths/adaptive timesteps consume
    random draws differently. These are single-realization sensitivity tests,
    not paired trajectories or statistical confidence intervals. Small changes
    should not be interpreted as established accuracy improvements.
